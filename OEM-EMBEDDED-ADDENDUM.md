# RIGOR-X OEM and Embedded Distribution Addendum

**Addendum version:** 1.0 · **Date:** 2026-11-25

This addendum applies only when expressly incorporated into an executed Agreement and Order Form. It supplements the [RIGOR-X Commercial Subscription License](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md). No OEM, embedded, redistribution, or white-label rights arise without that express incorporation.

## 1. Eligible commercial models

This addendum is intended for original equipment manufacturers, embedded software vendors, product integrators, commercial distributors, device and appliance manufacturers, enterprise product companies, and authorized white-label providers.

## 2. Permitted integration and distribution

Subject to the Order Form, the Licensor authorizes the Licensee to:
- integrate specified RIGOR-X components into identified Licensee products, devices, software, appliances, or packaged solutions;
- compile, package, install, and distribute approved object-code components solely as embedded elements of those products;
- distribute approved runtime dependencies, connectors, and integration artifacts only to the extent listed in the Order Form;
- provide product updates, maintenance, and support that include authorized RIGOR-X components; and
- apply approved white-label or co-branding arrangements where the Order Form specifies branding, attribution, notices, and customer-facing representations.

The Order Form must identify product families, component versions, distribution channels, territories (if relevant), unit or deployment limits, production volumes, permitted customer classes, and any source-code escrow or special support arrangements.

## 3. Integration versus redistribution of RIGOR-X itself

Permission to embed RIGOR-X in a product is not permission to distribute RIGOR-X as a standalone product, source repository, developer platform, general-purpose SDK, or independently reusable software package.

The Licensee may distribute only the specific approved object-code or integration components as inseparable or purpose-limited elements of the licensed product. It must not provide source code, build scripts, internal architecture, proprietary documentation, or reusable core components to customers or distributors unless expressly listed in the Order Form.

A recipient's right is limited to operating the licensed product as supplied. It does not include independent rights to extract, modify, reuse, sublicense, or commercially exploit RIGOR-X.

## 4. White-label arrangements

White-label rights must be explicit. The Order Form should state whether RIGOR-X branding may be removed, whether attribution is required in legal notices or technical documentation, which marks may be used, and whether the Licensee may market the resulting product under its own brand. No trademark or endorsement right is implied.

## 5. Licensee obligations

The Licensee must:
- preserve required proprietary and third-party notices;
- ensure distributors, resellers, and customers are bound by restrictions that protect Licensor Materials;
- prevent extraction or standalone redistribution of RIGOR-X;
- maintain a record of authorized product versions and distribution volumes;
- obtain all third-party component and model rights required for the product;
- implement reasonable product security and update controls; and
- report suspected unauthorized redistribution or material security compromise.

## 6. Source access and modifications

Source-code access, modification rights, derivative-work arrangements, escrow, maintenance access, and ownership of commissioned modifications must be expressly stated in the Order Form or a separate signed schedule. No source delivery or assignment is implied by an OEM or embedded grant.

## 7. Fees, reporting, and audit

The Order Form may use per-unit royalties, deployment fees, production-volume bands, monthly minimums, usage charges, or other negotiated metrics. It must specify reporting cadence, records, verification rights, and any reasonable audit process. No price or royalty is fixed by this addendum.

## 8. Termination and product wind-down

The Order Form must define treatment of already-manufactured inventory, customer support, security updates, and product wind-down on expiry or termination. Unless expressly authorized, the Licensee must stop new distribution and new activations when the license ends. Any sell-off period or perpetual end-user operation right must be stated expressly and cannot be inferred.
