# Licensing Changelog

All dates use ISO 8601 calendar dates. This log records published licensing-document revisions; it does not amend an executed Agreement by itself.

## 2026-11-25 — CSL 1.0

### Added
- Proprietary, source-available repository notice; no standard open-source license grant.
- Modular RIGOR-X Commercial Subscription License framework.
- Extensible category profiles and negotiated Order Form mechanism.
- Monthly base subscription plus configurable usage-based fees and optional add-ons.
- Subscription terms for billing, renewal, cancellation, overage, suspension, grace periods, expiry, export, and post-termination.
- Platform-provider addendum for hosted AI services and authorized downstream functionality.
- OEM/embedded addendum for scoped integration, product distribution, and white-label deployments.
- Versioning, amendment, renewal, and grandfathering rules that protect existing signed agreements from automatic retroactive revision.
